# Security policy

Meet Soundboard runs inside Google Meet and touches your microphone, so security reports are welcome.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report it privately through [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/fricha05/meet-soundboard/security/advisories/new), not in a public issue. Include the steps to reproduce and what an attacker could achieve.

You should get an answer within a week. Only the latest version on `main` is supported.

## Scope

In scope: anything that lets a web page, a Meet add-on or another extension control the soundboard, read or alter the microphone stream, or reach data stored by the extension.

Out of scope: the content of the bundled sounds and the availability of myinstants.com.
